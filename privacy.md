# Hypey privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Hypey runs on your iPhone. We don't run servers that receive your data, and we don't have accounts.

Camera and microphone. Used only while you're live, so your AI fans can react to what they see and hear. Video and audio are analysed on your device by Apple's on-device frameworks and are never uploaded, stored by us or shared.

Speech. Turned into text on your device by Apple's speech recognition so the chat can reply. The text is kept in memory for about 30 seconds during a live and then discarded.

AI chat. Written on your device by Apple's on-device language model. Nothing is sent to us or any AI provider.

Recordings. If you record a live, the clip is saved on your iPhone. It only leaves your phone if you choose to share it or save it to Photos.

Profile photo and settings. Your name, photo and audience settings are stored on your iPhone only.

Purchases. Handled by Apple. We receive no payment details.

Notifications. Used only for trial reminders, scheduled on your device.

Analytics. Hypey uses Aptabase, a privacy-first analytics service, to count anonymous app events such as "a live ended" or "onboarding finished", with coarse numbers like how long a live lasted. Events never include your camera, voice, chat, photos, name or settings text, and carry no advertising identifier or anything that identifies you. Aptabase also records non-identifying context such as app version, device model, OS version and country. There are no ads and no tracking across other apps or websites.

Children. Hypey is not directed at children under 13.
