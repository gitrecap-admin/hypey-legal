# Hypey Support

Hypey is a fake live stream simulator: go live to an AI audience that watches, listens and chats back, all on your iPhone.

## Frequently asked questions

**The fans don't reply to what I say.**
AI fans that listen and reply need Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or newer) turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. Also allow Hypey to use the microphone and speech recognition: Settings → Apps → Hypey. On other iPhones Hypey runs with classic fans, which react to your live but don't answer questions.

**The chat is in the wrong language.**
In Hypey, tap Audience on the home screen and pick the chat language. AI fans can write in the languages Apple's on-device model supports; in other languages they chat in English.

**My camera is black.**
Allow camera access: Settings → Apps → Hypey → Camera. Without the camera, your live still runs on your voice.

**Where is my recorded clip?**
Turn on "Record my lives" on the home screen. When you end a live, the clip is on the summary screen: tap Post clip or Save to keep it in Photos.

**Is anything uploaded?**
No. The camera, your voice and the chat are processed on your iPhone and never uploaded. Hypey has no accounts and no ads. Anonymous usage counts are described in the [privacy policy](./privacy.md).

**How does the free trial work?**
Hypey is free for 3 days. Nothing is charged when the trial ends. After that, a single one-time purchase unlocks it forever, with no subscription.

**I bought the lifetime unlock but it shows as locked.**
Open the paywall in Hypey and tap **Restore**. Make sure you're signed in with the same App Store account you bought with.

**Are the viewers real?**
No. Hypey is a simulator for fun and practice. Viewers, comments and gifts are generated on your device.
